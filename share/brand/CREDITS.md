# Brand assets

The icon (`share/icons/`) and the wordmarks here carry letterforms outlined from
[Fraunces](https://github.com/undercasetype/Fraunces), SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`OFL-Fraunces.txt`).
Only outlines are stored, not the font. `scripts/make-app-icon.py` rebuilds them.
`fugue-icon-small.svg` is the heavier cut the 16 to 64 px PNGs are rendered from.
