# Window placement

Window UI, changed or new, follows this file. [canvas-look.md](canvas-look.md) and [spec.md](spec.md) win when they name the same control.

The dated audit [.ui-ergonomics/2026-11-20-main-window.md](../.ui-ergonomics/2026-11-20-main-window.md) is the measurement of the main window, the Run menu, and node search (from source, text size 12). This file is the rule set that measurement produced, and the list a later change keeps current.

## Rules

Apply these to every control this change adds or moves. The procedure for a control the list below does not settle is the `ui-ergonomics` skill (`references/constraints.md`, `motor.md`, `search.md`, `grouping.md`).

- **Floor.** The press target is at least 24px on its smaller side. A smaller target names its exception in the **Decisions** row: the size is essential (a port on the 6u row), or a 24px circle centered on it meets no other target.
- **Primary.** Run on Ctrl+Enter is the main action (spec 12.3). It stays a key on this window, and the Run menu stays the pointer method. A new surface has one primary control, with a treatment the other controls on that surface do not share.
- **Destructive.** Its own chunk. The gap before it is at least twice the gap inside the neighboring chunk. It is not the last row of a menu. A reversible action keeps undo on this window.
- **Gaps.** One gap inside a chunk. The gap between chunks is at least twice that, in `u`. A label sits closer to its own control than to the control before it.
- **Learned place.** A menu order, or a command the spec marks as matching ComfyUI, stays where it is. Size and gap may change. A reorder waits until that row moves from **Not scheduled** to **Open**.
- **Posture.** The canvas is sovereign: keep its density, and raise a frequent target or anything under the floor. A dialog or a popover is transient: fewer decisions, and its primary target may be larger.
- **Same surface.** The steps of Run stay on this window.

## References

Blender and Photoshop are the usability references for the creative surfaces (owner decision, 2026-11-22): their conventions have years of refinement, and people arrive knowing them. A new creative surface (the Canvas view, its tool strip, layers, masks, areas and workspaces) starts from what they do, and the **Decisions** row names the convention it follows and any place it differs and why.

- **Take:** the interaction patterns and keys (a left tool strip, a properties area beside the work, layers with an eye, opacity, blend mode and a mask thumbnail, hover and F2 rename, drag-number fields, area headers with an editor-type menu, workspaces as saved layouts, Q for a quick mask, Space to pan, alt-click the eye to solo).
- **Do better where they are rough:** Blender's learning curve and Photoshop's density. The default stays the simple one-area window (hobbyists); the depth sits behind opt-in workspaces and advanced settings (film teams). Fugue's own rules above still win when they conflict (floor, primary, destructive, gaps).
- **Never copy assets or code:** no icons, artwork, names used as branding, or source. Blender's code is GPL, so only its manual and developer documentation are read; Photoshop is read from Adobe's public documentation.
- **Keys:** adopt their key where the command registry has no owner for it; a collision is settled in the Decisions row, never by silently taking the key.

## On a change or a new surface

1. Read **Rules**, **Open**, and **Not scheduled**, and the canvas-look or spec section for each control you add or move.
2. Each of those controls meets **Rules**. A **Not scheduled** row for that control stays as written.
3. An **Open** row for a control this change touches is finished in this change.
4. Record the control under **Decisions**: file, old value, new value, and the rule it meets.
5. When the decision belongs in the look or the spec, write it there and leave a pointer in the row.

Done when every added or moved control has a **Decisions** row that names the rule it meets, any **Open** row for a control this change touches has moved to **Decisions**, and every **Not scheduled** row is unchanged.

## Cohesion checklist

Before a new surface or control is done, find the long-standing surface that does the same job and check each line against it. The audit these lines come from, with the reference table and the open findings: [.ui-ergonomics/2026-11-22-canvas-areas-cohesion.md](../.ui-ergonomics/2026-11-22-canvas-areas-cohesion.md).

- [ ] **Components.** An existing component is reused: `ViewButton` (the one button; `SmallButton` and the area header's buttons are its variants, D4), `SettingsParts` (`SmallButton`, `Toggle`, `Setting`), `Question`, `LargeViewDialog`, `WidgetMenu`, `CropFrame`, `CompareView`, `CompareModeButtons`, `Banner`, `ContextBox`, `NewCanvasDialog`, `canvasMenu.js`, `ValueSlider`, `Glyph`, `FugueScrollBar`. A private copy (a button, a mode list, a file dialog, a menu's rows) is a finding; extend the component instead.
- [ ] **Tokens.** No color, font size or radius typed as a literal: colors from `look.*`, sizes in `u`, corners from `look.smallCorner` or `look.popupCorner`, edges 1px `look.hairline`, glyphs 14px. A literal over a picture (white, a dark halo) is the same one `CropFrame` uses.
- [ ] **One primary.** Only the primary is filled with the accent. A toggle or a current choice on that surface uses the quiet marking (`accented: false`), never the accent fill (D7). Never two primaries on screen at once, even in two parts of one surface.
- [ ] **Destructive.** `danger` tint, its own chunk, last in its row, the gap before it at least twice the row's gap. A reversible removal is tinted too. A question about a loss highlights the safe answer (Cancel, Keep), and the destructive answer is never the default (D8). A question's answers are `ThemedButton`s (the `ViewButton` alias) in a `QuestionAnswers` row, never a Material `Button` or a row of one's own (#235).
- [ ] **Order.** Cancel or Back first, the primary last at the right, as the Crop window and the questions (D9).
- [ ] **Words.** The same action has the same verb everywhere (Accept, Drop, Export…, OK for a commit that closes); a button is a verb; an ellipsis only when a dialog or file picker follows; sentence case; status words capitalized (Current, Missing, Candidate); Head capitalized as a name; a node by its title, never its id; "Canvas" is the picture, "graph" the node view, in code names too only where a person reads them (D10); **candidate** for a run's pending result, never "result" or "fill" (D3).
- [ ] **Glyphs.** Lucide from the subset (`share/fonts/lucide-fugue.txt`), never a text "×", "▾" or "‹"; a glyph means one thing in the whole window.
- [ ] **States.** Hover, pressed, focus (the 2px accent ring), disabled (dimmed, with a tip saying why: `ViewButton` shows it on hover even though Qt gives a disabled item no hover, #281) and error (`look.error`) as `ViewButton` draws them; a toggle tells assistive tools it is on.
- [ ] **Keys.** One keymap for a picture and the graph: Home fits, 1 is 100%, + and - zoom, Escape closes or cancels, Enter takes the surface's primary when the focus is not in a text field (D5); a new key is checked against the registry and the References, and a tooltip names it.
- [ ] **Keyboard.** Every control and list row is reached by Tab or the arrows, in reading order.
- [ ] **Right-to-left.** A popup sets `LayoutMirroring` on its content (it is outside the window's mirrored root); a picture is never mirrored.
- [ ] **Empty and error.** An empty state says what to do in the app (a button), not on the command line; the same state has the same words everywhere.
- [ ] **No technical text around the work.** No internal text over or under the work area unless the person needs it to act on: sizes, zoom and positions go in one thin status line (Photoshop's); how-to lines are tooltips; the details a pro wants sit behind an Advanced toggle or a tooltip, never a paragraph (#234).
- [ ] **Pictures.** A `FUGUE_SHOT_DIR` picture of the surface, light and dark, looked at beside the reference surface's; narrow width too for anything an area can host.

The D numbers are the audit's decisions of 2026-11-22. D4 (one button: `ViewButton` with its `bordered`, `flat`, `compact`, `primary`, `danger`, `checkable` variants; `SmallButton` and the area header's `BarButton` are thin aliases) and D5 (one keymap: Home or 0 fits a picture and the Graph, `+` and `-` zoom both, Enter takes the primary; a focused Canvas view keeps the Graph's keys from the Graph) are built (second batch, below); a new button or key extends them, never a copy.

## Not scheduled

Drafts from the 2026-11-20 pass that the rules above already answered. A window change leaves these as they are until the owner moves the row to **Open**.

| Control | As it is | Why it stays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delete on the selection toolbar | 2px from the neighboring buttons | An 8px gap is below what a person notices |
| Run-error row | height is the line plus 8px (22 for one line at text size 12) | The floor would make one line 24 |
| Run menu title | the same treatment as the other titles | Spec 12.3: the main action is Ctrl+Enter |
| Slider click | opens the editor; a drag sets the value | The same click as the other wells |
| Delete in the node menu | last row of `menus.cpp` `node()` | Spec 12.4 keeps the ComfyUI order |
| Resize corner | 4u, and at least 12 screen px | The size written in canvas-look §4 |
| Selection toolbar | one row of glyphs | canvas-look §11, the ComfyUI toolbox |

## Decisions

### 2026-11-24 Selection Brush; the Brush paints masks only (#380, epic #216)

- Control: `src/canvas/CanvasView.qml`: the tool strip's `canvasViewTool-selbrush` (glyph `paintbrush`, 24px like its neighbours, in the lasso's chunk) and the command `canvas-view.tool-selection-brush` (key A); the live red of `canvasViewSelection` while it strokes, `canvasViewLiveRect` while a rectangle is dragged, the lasso's fill (`BrushPen.liveTint`); the layer mask's overlay tint (`canvasViewLayerMask`, now the accent, not red); Quick Mask's overlay on whenever Q is turned on. The model is `src/canvas/paint_target.h` (`PaintTarget`).
- Old: the Brush painted the selection when Quick Mask or the lasso was on and a layer's mask otherwise, so one tool painted two things by a mode that was easy to miss; the painted selection showed nothing until release (ants), and Quick Mask's red was behind a toggle that started off.
- New: three painters, each with one target. The Selection Brush paints the selection (Alt at the press subtracts, `[` and `]` size it) and shows the covered area live as 40% red; on release the ants take over. The Brush and Eraser paint the selected layer's mask only (Quick Mask excepted, below), with their own live trace in the mask's tint (accent) even when the Overlay is off. Quick Mask (Q) stays Photoshop's toggle: any brush paints the selection while it is on, and its red overlay is on at every entry (`\` hides it). The lasso and a dragged rectangle fill their area in the same red while the drag is on.
- Why: Photoshop Elements' Selection Brush and Photoshop's Quick Mask painting are the convention (References): "what the next action affects" (the selection, red) and "what a layer hides" (the mask) each get their own tool and colour. Photoshop's mask overlay is also red; Fugue differs on purpose, so the two never look alike (the owner asked for the split). Photoshop Elements' Selection Brush has Alt for subtract too; Alt+drag no longer sizes this brush (the other brushes keep it).
- Rules met: Floor (the strip button is the strip's 24px), Primary (none added), Destructive (nothing removed; every stroke is a selection undo step), Gaps (the strip's own), Keys (A had no owner in the registry; a tooltip names it), Glyphs (`paintbrush` added to the Lucide subset, means this tool only).

### 2026-11-23 Layers panel: groups as a tree (#278, epics #206 and #216)

- Control: `src/canvas/CanvasLayersPanel.qml`: the arrow `canvasLayerArrow-<position>` at the left of a group's row (flat compact `ViewButton`, 24px), the rows of an open group's layers (`canvasLayer-<position>` as before, indented), the Left and Right keys on `canvasLayerList`, the drop line `canvasLayerDropLine` (error color when the layers cannot land there), the folder glyph on a group inside a group, the row menu's top-level-only entries. The tree logic is `src/engine/canvas_layer_tree.h` (`LayerOutline`), the rows come from `CanvasViewModel::layers`.
- Old: a group was one row; its layers could only be reached by Ungroup, and `depth` was always 0.
- New: a group's row has an arrow (Photoshop's and Krita's disclosure triangle, Blender's outliner arrow) in a 6u column that exists only while the canvas has a group, so a flat canvas looks as before. A click opens or closes it; Right opens a closed group (again: into it), Left closes an open one (again: out to its group); Alt+click on an arrow opens or closes every group (Photoshop's Alt+click on the arrow). Groups start closed; the open state is kept by names, so it survives edits and renames. Layers inside are indented 3u a level. A hidden group dims what is inside it (the children's own eye keeps its own state and comes back with the group), a locked group protects its layers (the lock shows lit and dim on them, with the reason in its tip). A closing group takes the selection of the layers it hides, as Photoshop does. Edits work inside a group as at the top level: eye, lock, opacity, blend, rename, delete, duplicate, move, New layer (above the current one, in its group), Group and Ungroup; each is one revision. A Shift or Ctrl pick across groups is changed one group at a time (the panel says so). A drag moves layers within their group; a line elsewhere is red and the drop says why.
- Why: the owner's Photoshop model with Blender's interface (References); Photoshop and Krita show a group as a row with an arrow and indented children, and Blender's outliner dims the children of a hidden parent. The research is from their manuals as known; Photoshop's and Blender's pages could not be re-fetched in this session. Hick: groups are closed until wanted. Fitts: a 24px arrow at the row's edge, keys for the keyboard.
- Rules met: Floor (arrow 24px), Primary (none added), Destructive (nothing removed; every edit one revision with Undo), Gaps (1u inside a row as before), Learned place (the eye stays at the right edge, the thumbnails keep their place), Posture (dense: no extra rows or notices), Keys (Left and Right are the list's own, no command owns them). Deviations: the drag cannot move a layer into or out of a group yet (#278 stays open for it); Merge down, Flatten, Move and Free Transform, the mask brush, selections from a layer and Generate's layer read the top level only (the menu rows are disabled for a layer inside a group, the model says why); a layer inside a group cannot be shown alone.
